Statutory Clean Energy Policy

The Legal Game-Changer: 26 U.S.C. Section 6417

Internal Revenue Code Elective Pay (Direct Pay) allows tax-exempt political subdivisions to receive direct cash reimbursement checks from the U.S. Treasury.

Treasury Decision 9988 Statutory Mechanics

Finalized by the IRS in March 2024, TD 9988 governs elective pay procedures for public school districts:

C_net = C_turnkey - (C_turnkey * R_IRA)
Where R_IRA is the statutory additive direct cash reimbursement rate (30% to 50%).
