Policy Revolution

The Game-Changer: IRA Section 6417 'Direct Pay'

For the first time in American history, public schools receive upfront cash reimbursement checks from the IRS.

How Direct Pay Works for Public Schools

Under IRS Section 6417 enacted in the 2022 Inflation Reduction Act, tax-exempt government entities (school districts) bypass private tax-equity middlemen and receive an uncapped direct cash refund check directly from the U.S. Treasury.

1

IRS Pre-Filing Electronic Registration

District submits planned solar canopy project to receive an IRS electronic registration number.

2

File Annual Form 990-T

District reports capital expenditure and attaches IRS Form 3468 clean energy investment schedule.

3

Electronic Direct Deposit or Check

IRS issues a cash reimbursement check covering 30% to 50% of the turnkey installation cost.

Beyond Clean Power

Multi-Dimensional Campus Co-Benefits

Parking lot canopies solve multiple operational and educational challenges simultaneously.

-30°F
Surface Microclimate Cooling

Canopies intercept direct solar radiation, lowering asphalt surface temperatures from 145°F down to ambient shade, drastically reducing the campus urban heat island effect.

EV Ready
Fleet Electrification Conduit

Canopy structural posts and underground trenches provide ready electrical conduit for Level 2 EV charging for teachers during the day and electric school buses overnight.

STEM Lab
Living Educational Dashboard

Real-time solar inverter generation data transforms the parking lot into a physical STEM learning laboratory for physics, climate science, and data engineering classes.

Why Not Rooftops? Rooftop solar voids 20-year roof warranties, requires roof penetrations, and has seismic load limits. Parking lots have zero roof penetration and 100% flat southern exposure.
